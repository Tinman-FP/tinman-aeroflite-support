TINMAN AEROFLITE

Authorized Use & Safety Terms

Effective September 30, 2026 · Version 1.0

Reference aid only

Tinman Aeroflite retrieves and summarizes approved documents. It is not operational authority and does not replace the current controlling manual, contract, regulation, checklist, or direction. AI output can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. Open and verify every cited source before acting.

Authorized access

Use is limited to people whose company email address has been approved by the Tinman Aeroflite administrator. Do not share an account, password, downloaded library, screenshots, citations, or document content with anyone who is not authorized to receive that material. Access may be disabled or revoked at any time. When access ends, sign out and remove the app and its downloaded content from the device.

Document and AI limitations

Document titles, revision labels, currency indicators, summaries, and citations can contain errors. The AI can omit qualifying language, misunderstand a question, or combine unrelated passages. When sources conflict, Tinman may display both positions but does not decide which source controls. Verify the complete cited pages and resolve any conflict with the responsible company, contracting officer, agency, pilot in command, maintenance authority, or other controlling authority as applicable.

No operational reliance

Do not rely on Tinman to conduct a flight, navigate, obtain weather or NOTAMs, calculate performance, release or dispatch an aircraft, approve maintenance, determine airworthiness, satisfy a required checklist, command an emergency response, or replace any required or approved publication. Use only current official sources and required operational systems for those purposes.

Do not interact with the app when doing so would distract from aircraft operation or interfere with required crewmember duties, sterile-cockpit procedures, company policy, or applicable law.

Updates and offline access

The app verifies account access, the application revision, document currency, and local downloads during preflight. Offline access expires no more than 24 hours after a successful online verification. Connect before use whenever updated information may exist. Content and model packages are data-only downloads; they cannot add executable features to the installed app.

Access grants only the limited right to use the material for authorized internal business reference. It does not grant permission to publish, sell, sublicense, reverse engineer protected content, or redistribute it.

Availability and responsibility

The service may be interrupted and results are provided without a guarantee of completeness or fitness for a particular operational purpose. Nothing in these terms limits rights or obligations that cannot lawfully be limited. Report access, security, currency, or content concerns to the Tinman Aeroflite administrator before relying on the affected material.
